How do you want to budget?

You can switch later without losing anything.
Category limits
A spending limit for each category, with optional rollover.
Good if you want to keep an eye on a few categories.
Zero-based
Give every dollar that comes in a job, until there’s nothing left to assign.
Good if you want a plan for all of it.
50/30/20
Split income into needs, wants and savings by percentage. Or your own split.
Good if you’d rather not budget category by category.
Flex
Budget fixed and non-monthly costs; everything else shares one number.
Good if you want one number to watch.
